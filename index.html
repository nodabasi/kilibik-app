Kılıbık

She writes the list, he ticks it off

A to-do list built for two

Kılıbık connects two phones. One of you takes the Prenses role and sets the tasks; the other takes the Erkek role and marks them done. Nothing more, nothing to configure — a shared list with a sense of humour about who is in charge.

How it works

  • Sign up and pick a sideRegister with your e-mail, confirm it, and choose Prenses or Erkek.
  • Pair with one codeOne of you generates a six-character invite code; the other types it in. A pair is only valid if both sides agree.
  • Set a taskThe Prenses writes a title, an optional detail and a little note. It lands on the other phone straight away.
  • Tap “Hallettim”The Erkek marks it done and it turns green on both phones at once.

Notifications

When a new task is created, the phone it was assigned to gets a push notification. That is the only notification the app sends — there is no marketing, no reminder spam and no daily digest.

Only the two of you

A task is visible to the person who wrote it and the person it was given to. Nobody else can read it, and the app has no way to browse other users or search for them — the only route to a pairing is a code that someone hands you.

Either side can end the pairing at any time. When they do, every task between the two is deleted for both of them.

Built to stay out of your way

  • Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
  • No analytics and no tracking
  • Light, dark or follow-the-system appearance
  • Delete your account yourself, from inside the app
  • Turkish interface
