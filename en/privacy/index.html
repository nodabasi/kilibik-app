Privacy Policy

What we store, and how to erase it

Effective 4 September 2026 · Applies to the Kılıbık mobile app for iOS and Android

The short version

Kılıbık needs an account, because the whole point is syncing a list between two phones. So unlike a purely offline app, we do hold your data on a server: your e-mail address, the name you chose, and the tasks you and your partner exchange.

We do not sell it, do not show ads, and run no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your tasks are readable by exactly two people — you and the partner you paired with. You can delete the entire account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Your e-mail address — used to sign you in, to verify the address at sign-up, and to reset your password. Your partner can see it on their Settings screen.
  • Your display name and role — the name you type at registration and whether you chose Prenses or Erkek. Both are shown to your partner.
  • Your pairing — who you are paired with, and the six-character invite code while it is waiting to be used. The code is deleted the moment it is redeemed.
  • Your tasks — the title, the optional detail and note, whether it is done, and the times it was created and completed.
  • A notification token for each device — an identifier Google gives us so a notification can reach your phone. It identifies the installation, not you.

That is the complete list. There is no location, no contacts, no photos, no address book, no advertising identifier and no device fingerprint.

Where it is stored

Everything sits in Google Firebase — Firebase Authentication for the account, Cloud Firestore for the profile and tasks, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for the notifications. That means the data is held on Google Cloud servers under Firebase's privacy terms, and Google acts as our processor.

Nobody else receives your data. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not handed to any third party beyond the Google services listed above.

Who can read your tasks

Two people: the one who created the task and the one it was assigned to. This is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app.

  • You can read your own profile and your partner's — nobody else's.
  • There is no way to list or search users. The only route into a pairing is a code somebody gives you.
  • A pairing counts only when both profiles point at each other, so a one-sided write grants nobody access.

We can technically reach the database as its administrator, as any operator can. We do not read your tasks, and we would only ever look at stored data if you asked us to while reporting a problem.

Notifications

When a task is created, we send one push notification to the phone it was assigned to. It contains the sender's name and the task title. That is the only notification Kılıbık sends — no marketing, no reminders, no digests.

You can turn notifications off in your phone's system settings at any time; the app keeps working without them.

Deleting your data

Two separate actions, both in Settings:

  • Eşleşmeyi bitir ends the pairing and deletes every task between you and your partner, on both sides. Your accounts remain.
  • Hesabımı sil deletes the account itself. Your tasks go, the pairing on your partner's side is cleared, your profile is removed and the login is destroyed.

Account deletion asks for your password first. That is a security requirement, not a formality — it confirms it is really you before anything irreversible happens.

Neither action can be undone, and we keep no backup copy to restore from.

If you cannot reach the in-app option for any reason, write to the address at the bottom of this page and we will delete the account for you.

How long we keep it

Your account and tasks stay until you delete them. There is no automatic expiry, and no archive kept after deletion.

No ads, no analytics

Kılıbık is free and carries no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service. Nothing about how you use the app is measured or reported anywhere.

App stores

Kılıbık is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google collect their own download and usage statistics as part of running those stores, under their own privacy policies. We receive only anonymous aggregate reports.

Children

Kılıbık is meant for adults in a relationship and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has registered, write to us and we will remove the account.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK give you the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. Your name and role are editable in the app, deletion is a button in Settings, and for anything else — including a copy of what we hold — write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app stores or transmits, this page is updated before that version is released, and the effective date above changes with it.
